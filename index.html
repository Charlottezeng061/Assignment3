How to use

  1. Click to preview

    Choose a genre and an atmosphere. Click a button to hear a 6-second preview.

  2. Double-click to change the sound

    Each button contains 4 sounds. Double-click the same button to try the next one. “Sound 1 / 4” means the first of four sounds.

    With a sound button focused, press → to try the next sound.

  3. Play your soundtrack

    Press Play to hear both layers together. Use the volume slider to adjust the sound level.

Random tries different sounds within your chosen genre and atmosphere. Repeat keeps both sounds looping. Reset restores the starting selections and volume.
